
The Gospel According To Mark


01:01 The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

01:02 As it is written in the Prophets, “Behold, I am sending my messenger ahead of you, who will prepare your way before you,

01:03 the voice of one crying out in the wilderness, ‘Prepare the way of the Lord; make his paths straight,’ ”

01:04 John came baptizing in the wilderness and preaching a baptism of repentance for the remission of sins.

01:05 The whole Judean countryside was going out to him, and so were the people of Jerusalem. They were all being baptized by him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.

01:06 Now John was clothed with camel’s hair and wore a leather belt around his waist; he ate locusts and wild honey.

01:07 He proclaimed, “After me comes one who is mightier than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and untie.

01:08 I have baptized you with water, but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.”


01:09 In those days Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan.

01:10 Immediately as he was coming up from the water, he saw the heavens being split apart and the Spirit descending on him like a dove.

01:11 Then a voice came from heaven: “You are my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”


01:12 Immediately the Spirit drove him out into the wilderness,

01:13 and he was there in the wilderness for forty days, being tempted by Satan. He was with the wild beasts, and the angels ministered to him.


01:14 After John was put in prison, Jesus went to Galilee, preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God

01:15 and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has drawn near. Repent and believe in the gospel.”


01:16 As Jesus was walking by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother (that is, Simon’s brother) casting a net in the sea, for they were fishermen.

01:17 Jesus said to them, “Follow me, and I will make you become fishers of men.”

01:18 So they immediately left their nets and followed him.

01:19 Going on a little farther from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother as they were in the boat mending their nets.

01:20 Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and followed him.


01:21 They went to Capernaum, and immediately on the Sabbath Jesus went into the synagogue and began teaching.

01:22 The people were astonished at his teaching, because he was teaching them as one who had authority, and not as the scribes.

01:23 Now in their synagogue there was a man who was possessed by an unclean spirit. He cried out,

01:24 “Leave us alone! What do you have to do with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are—the Holy One of God.”

01:25 But Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silent, and come out of him!”

01:26 So the unclean spirit convulsed the man and came out of him, crying out with a loud voice.

01:27 All the people were amazed so that they questioned among themselves, saying, “What is this? What new teaching is this that with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him?”

01:28 And the news about him immediately spread throughout all the surrounding region of Galilee.


01:29 After leaving the synagogue, they immediately went to the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.

01:30 Now Simon’s mother-in-law lay sick with a fever, and they immediately told Jesus about her.

01:31 So he went over to her and raised her up, taking hold of her hand. Immediately the fever left her, and she began to serve them.


01:32 When evening came, after the sun had set, they brought to Jesus all who were sick and those who were possessed by demons.

01:33 The whole city was gathered together at the door,

01:34 and he healed many who were sick with various diseases. He also cast out many demons, but he would not allow the demons to speak, because they knew who he was.


01:35 Early the next morning, while it was still very dark, Jesus rose and departed. He went off to a desolate place and prayed there.

01:36 Simon and his companions searched diligently for him,

01:37 and when they found him, they said to him, “Everyone is looking for you.”

01:38 Jesus said to them, “Let us go into the neighboring towns so that I may preach there also, for that is why I have come.”

01:39 So he was preaching in their synagogues in all of Galilee and casting out demons.


01:40 Then a leper came up and knelt before him, begging him, “If you are willing, you can make me clean.”

01:41 Moved with compassion, Jesus stretched out his hand, touched the man, and said to him, “I am willing; be made clean.”

01:42 When he said this, the leprosy immediately departed from the man, and he was made clean.

01:43 Then Jesus sternly warned him and immediately sent him away,

01:44 saying to him, “See that you say nothing to anyone, but go show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.”

01:45 But the man went out and began to proclaim it widely and to spread the news, so that Jesus could no longer openly enter a town, but was out in desolate places. Yet people were coming to him from everywhere.


02:01 A few days later Jesus went to Capernaum again, and the people heard that he was at home.

02:02 Immediately many were gathered together, so that there was no longer any room, not even at the door, and he was speaking the word to them.

02:03 Then some people came to him, bringing a paralytic carried by four men.

02:04 Since they were not able to come near Jesus because of the crowd, they uncovered the roof where he was. After digging through it, they let down the mat on which the paralytic was lying.

02:05 When Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralytic, “Son, your sins are forgiven you.”

02:06 Now some of the scribes were sitting there and questioning in their hearts:

02:07 “Why does this man speak such blasphemies? Who can forgive sins but God alone?”

02:08 Immediately Jesus knew in his spirit that they were discussing these questions among themselves, so he said to them, “Why are you questioning these things in your hearts?

02:09 Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Rise, take up your mat, and walk’?

02:10 But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”—he said to the paralytic—

02:11 “I say to you, rise, pick up your mat, and go to your house.”

02:12 So the man immediately rose, picked up his mat, and went out in front of them all, so that they were all amazed and began glorifying God, saying, “We have never seen anything like this!”


02:13 Once again Jesus went out by the sea. The entire crowd came to him, and he taught them.

02:14 As he was walking along, he saw Levi the son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax booth. Jesus said to him, “Follow me,” so Levi rose and followed him.

02:15 Later, as Jesus was reclining at the table in Levi’s house, many tax collectors and sinners reclined with him and his disciples, for there were many who followed him.

02:16 When the scribes and the Pharisees saw Jesus eating with tax collectors and sinners, they said to his disciples, “Why is he eating and drinking with tax collectors and sinners?”

02:17 When Jesus heard this, he said to them, “It is not those who are healthy who have need of a physician, but those who are sick. I did not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”


02:18 Now the disciples of John and the disciples of the Pharisees were fasting. So some people came and said to Jesus, “Why do the disciples of John and the disciples of the Pharisees fast, but your disciples do not fast?”

02:19 Jesus said to them, “Can the bridegroom’s attendants fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them they cannot fast.

02:20 But the days are coming when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast in those days.

02:21 “No one sews a piece of unshrunk cloth on an old garment. Otherwise, the garment’s new patch pulls away from the old cloth, and a worse tear is made.

02:22 And no one puts new wine into old wineskins. Otherwise, the new wine bursts the wineskins, and the wine is spilled, and the wineskins are destroyed. But new wine must be put into new wineskins.”


02:23 On the Sabbath Jesus was going through the grainfields, and his disciples began to make their way, plucking heads of grain.

02:24 So the Pharisees said to him, “Behold, why are they doing what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath?”

02:25 He said to them, “Have you never read what David did when he had need and was hungry, he and those who were with him,

02:26 how he entered the house of God, in the time of Abiathar the high priest, and ate the bread of the Presence, which is not lawful for anyone to eat except for the priests, and how he also gave it to those who were with him?”

02:27 Then he said to them, “The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath.

02:28 So the Son of Man is Lord even of the Sabbath.”


03:01 Once again Jesus went into the synagogue, and a man was there who had a withered hand.

03:02 Now the Pharisees were watching Jesus closely to see if he would heal the man on the Sabbath, so that they could accuse him.

03:03 And he said to the man with the withered hand, “Rise and come forward.”

03:04 Then Jesus said to them, “Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill?” But they were silent.

03:05 After looking around at them with anger, grieved by the hardness of their hearts, he said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” So he stretched it out, and his hand was restored, as sound as the other.

03:06 Then the Pharisees went out and immediately took counsel with the Herodians against Jesus as to how they might destroy him.


03:07 But Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the sea, and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and also from Judea,

03:08 from Jerusalem, from Idumea, and from beyond the Jordan. A great multitude of people from around Tyre and Sidon also came to him when they heard about everything he was doing.

03:09 Then he told his disciples to have a small boat ready for him, so that the crowd would not crush him.

03:10 For he had healed many, so that all who had afflictions were pressing toward him to touch him.

03:11 Whenever the unclean spirits saw him, they would fall down before him and cry out, “You are the Son of God.”

03:12 But he would strictly warn them not to make him known.


03:13 Afterward Jesus went up on the mountain and called for those whom he wanted, and they came to him.

03:14 He appointed twelve so that they might be with him and so that he might send them out to preach

03:15 and to have authority to heal diseases and cast out demons.

03:16 He appointed Simon (whom he gave the name Peter);

03:17 James the son of Zebedee and John the brother of James (whom he gave the name Boanerges, which means, Sons of Thunder);

03:18 Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Cananite,

03:19 and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed him.


Then they entered a house,

03:20 and a crowd came together again, so that Jesus and his disciples could not even eat bread.

03:21 When his family heard about it, they went out to restrain him, for they said, “He is out of his mind.”

03:22 But the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem said, “He has Beelzebul,” and, “By the ruler of demons he casts out demons.”

03:23 So Jesus called them over and said to them in parables, “How can Satan cast out Satan?

03:24 If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.

03:25 And if a house is divided against itself, that house cannot stand.

03:26 And if Satan has risen up against himself and is divided, he cannot stand, but has come to an end.

03:27 No one can enter the house of a strong man and plunder his goods unless he first binds the strong man. Then he can plunder his house.

03:28 “Truly I say to you, all sins will be forgiven the sons of men, and whatever blasphemies they utter.

03:29 But whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness, but is subject to eternal judgment.”

03:30 He said this because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”


03:31 Then Jesus’ brothers and mother came, and standing outside, they sent word to him and called for him.

03:32 There was a crowd sitting around Jesus, and they said to him, “Behold, your mother, your brothers, and your sisters are outside looking for you.”

03:33 But he answered them, “Who is my mother, or my brothers?”

03:34 And looking around in a circle at the people sitting there, he said, “Behold, these are my mother and my brothers!

03:35 For whoever does the will of God is my brother and my sister and mother.”


04:01 Once again Jesus began to teach by the sea, and a large crowd was gathered around him. So he got into the boat and sat in it on the sea, while the entire crowd was beside the sea on the shore.

04:02 Then he taught them many things in parables, and in his teaching he said to them,

04:03 “Listen! A sower went out to sow.

04:04 As he sowed, some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and devoured it.

04:05 Other seed fell on rocky ground, where it did not have much soil, and it sprang up immediately because it had no depth of soil.

04:06 But when the sun rose, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.

04:07 Other seed fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it, and it produced no fruit.

04:08 But other seed fell into good soil and produced fruit that grew and increased; some bore thirty, some sixty, and some a hundred times more than what was sown.”

04:09 Then he said, “He who has ears to hear, let him hear.”


04:10 When he was alone, those who were around him, along with the twelve, asked him about the parable.

04:11 So he said to them, “To you it has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God, but to those who are outside everything is said in parables,

04:12 so that ‘they may see but not perceive, and hear but not understand, lest they should turn back and their sins be forgiven them.’ ”

04:13 Then he said to them, “Do you not understand this parable? How then will you understand all the other parables?

04:14 The sower sows the word.

04:15 Now some people are like the seed along the path, where the word is sown. When they hear, Satan immediately comes and takes away the word that is sown in their hearts.

04:16 Likewise, some people are like the seed sown on rocky ground. When they hear the word, they immediately receive it with joy.

04:17 Yet they have no root in themselves, but are only temporary. When tribulation or persecution arises on account of the word, they immediately fall away.

04:18 Others are like the seed sown among the thorns. They hear the word,

04:19 but the cares of this world, the deceitfulness of riches, and the desire for other things enter in and choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.

04:20 But others are like the seed sown on the good soil. They hear the word, receive it, and bear fruit—some thirty, some sixty, and some a hundred times more than what was sown.”


04:21 He also said to them, “Is a lamp brought in to be put under a basket, or under a bed? Is it not brought in to be set on a lampstand?

04:22 For there is nothing hidden that will not be made manifest, nor has anything been made secret, but that it may come to light.

04:23 If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.”

04:24 Then he said to them, “Consider what you hear. With the measure you use it will be measured to you, and more will be added to you who hear.

04:25 For whoever has will be given more, but whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.”


04:26 He also said, “The kingdom of God is like a man who scatters seed on the ground.

04:27 He sleeps and rises night and day, and the seed sprouts and grows; he does not know how.

04:28 For the earth produces a crop by itself, first the stalk, then the head, then the full grain in the head.

04:29 When the crop is ready, the man immediately sends in the sickle, for the harvest has come.”


04:30 Then Jesus said, “To what can we compare the kingdom of God? Or what parable can we use to describe it?

04:31 It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown on the ground, is smaller than all the seeds on earth.

04:32 Yet when it is sown, it grows and becomes larger than all the garden plants and produces large branches, so that the birds of the sky can nest in its shade.”

04:33 With many similar parables he spoke the word to them, to the extent that they were able to understand it.

04:34 He did not speak to them without using a parable, but privately he explained everything to his disciples.


04:35 On that day, when evening came, Jesus said to his disciples, “Let us cross over to the other side of the sea.”

04:36 So they left the crowd and took him with them in the boat, just as he was. Other little boats were also with him.

04:37 Now a great windstorm arose, and the waves were beating against the boat, so that it was nearly swamped.

04:38 But Jesus was in the stern, asleep on a cushion. So they woke him up and said to him, “Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing?”

04:39 Then he woke up and rebuked the wind and said to the sea, “Peace! Be still!” So the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.

04:40 Then he said to them, “Why are you so afraid? How is it that you have no faith?”

04:41 And they were filled with great fear and said to one another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?”


05:01 Then they came to the other side of the sea, to the region of the Gadarenes.

05:02 As Jesus got out of the boat, he was immediately met by a man from the tombs who had an unclean spirit.

05:03 This man lived among the tombs, and no one could bind him, not even with chains.

05:04 For he had often been bound with shackles and chains, but he tore the chains apart and broke the shackles in pieces. No one was strong enough to subdue him.

05:05 Night and day, on the mountains and among the tombs, he would continually cry out and cut himself with stones.

05:06 When he saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and bowed down before him.

05:07 Then he cried out with a loud voice and said, “What do you have to do with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I adjure you by God, do not torment me.”

05:08 For Jesus had said to him, “Come out of the man, you unclean spirit!”

05:09 Then Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” He answered, “My name is Legion, for we are many.”

05:10 And he earnestly begged Jesus not to send them out of that region.

05:11 Now there was a large herd of pigs feeding there near the mountain.

05:12 All the demons begged Jesus, “Send us into the pigs, so that we may go into them.”

05:13 So he immediately gave them permission, and the unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs. Then the herd, about two thousand in number, rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and were drowned in the sea.

05:14 Those who had been feeding the pigs then ran off and reported it in the city and in the countryside. So the people went out to see what had happened.

05:15 They came to Jesus and saw the man who had been possessed by demons sitting there, clothed and in his right mind—the man who had been possessed by the “Legion”—and they were afraid.

05:16 Those who had seen it told them what had happened to the man who had been possessed by demons, and they also told them about the pigs.

05:17 Then the people began begging Jesus to depart from their region.

05:18 When Jesus got into the boat, the man who had been possessed by demons begged to go with him.

05:19 But Jesus did not permit him to do so. Instead, he said to him, “Go to your house and to your people, and report to them all that the Lord has done for you and how he has had mercy on you.”

05:20 So the man went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis all that Jesus had done for him, and all the people were amazed.


05:21 When Jesus had again crossed over in the boat to the other side of the sea, a large crowd was gathered around him, and he was by the sea.

05:22 And behold, one of the rulers of the synagogue came, whose name was Jairus. When he saw Jesus, he fell down at his feet

05:23 and begged him earnestly, “My little daughter is at the point of death. Please come and lay your hands on her so that she may be healed, and she will live.”

05:24 So Jesus went with him, and a large crowd followed him and pressed in on him.

05:25 Now there was a woman who had suffered from a flow of blood for twelve years.

05:26 She had suffered a great deal under many physicians and had spent all she had, yet received no benefit from it, but had only grown worse.

05:27 When she heard about Jesus, she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his garment.

05:28 For she said, “If I touch even his garments, I will be healed.”

05:29 Immediately her flow of blood was dried up, and she knew in her body that she had been healed from her affliction.

05:30 Jesus immediately perceived in himself that power had gone out from him, so he turned around in the crowd and said, “Who touched my garments?”

05:31 His disciples said to him, “You see the crowd pressing in on you, and yet you say, ‘Who touched me?’ ”

05:32 But he kept looking around to see who had done it.

05:33 Now the woman was frightened and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, so she came and fell down before him and told him the whole truth.

05:34 Then Jesus said to her, “Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace, and be healed of your affliction.”

05:35 While he was still speaking, some people came from the ruler of the synagogue’s house and said, “Your daughter has died; why trouble the teacher any further?”

05:36 But when Jesus heard what they said, he immediately said to the ruler of the synagogue, “Do not be afraid; only believe.”

05:37 From that point he did not allow anyone to accompany him except Peter, James, and John the brother of James.

05:38 When he came to the ruler of the synagogue’s house, he saw a commotion, people weeping and wailing loudly.

05:39 Then he went in and said to them, “Why are you making a commotion and weeping? The child is not dead but sleeping.”

05:40 And they began laughing at him. But he put them all outside, took the child’s father and mother and those who were with him, and went in to where the child was lying down.

05:41 Then he took hold of the child’s hand and said to her, “Talitha koumi,” which means, “Little girl, I say to you, arise.”

05:42 Immediately the girl got up and began to walk (for she was twelve years old), and they were overcome with great amazement.

05:43 Then he gave them strict orders not to let anyone know about this, and he told them to give her something to eat.


06:01 Then Jesus left that place and went to his hometown, and his disciples followed him.

06:02 When the Sabbath came, he began to teach in the synagogue, and many who heard him were astonished, saying, “Where did this man get these things? And what is this wisdom that has been given to him, that such miracles are done by his hands?

06:03 Is this not the carpenter, the son of Mary, and the brother of James, Joses, Judas, and Simon? Are not his sisters here with us?” And they took offense at him.

06:04 Then Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor except in his hometown, among his relatives, and in his own household.”

06:05 So he could do no miracle there, except that he laid his hands on a few sick people and healed them.

06:06 And he was amazed at their unbelief.


Then Jesus went around among the villages teaching.

06:07 He summoned the twelve and began to send them out two by two, giving them authority over unclean spirits.

06:08 He instructed them to take nothing for their journey except a staff—no knapsack, no bread, and no copper money in their belts.

06:09 He told them to wear sandals but not an extra tunic.

06:10 He also said to them, “Whenever you enter a house, stay there until you leave that place.

06:11 If anyone will not receive you or listen to you, shake the dust off your feet as you leave that place, as a testimony against them. Truly I say to you, it will be more tolerable for Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that city.”

06:12 So they went out and preached that people should repent.

06:13 They also cast out many demons and anointed with oil many who were sick and healed them.


06:14 Now King Herod heard about this, for Jesus’ name had become known, and he said, “John the Baptist has been raised from the dead, and that is why these mighty powers are at work in him.”

06:15 Others said, “He is Elijah.” And others said, “He is a prophet, like one of the prophets.”

06:16 But when Herod heard this, he said, “This is John, whom I beheaded; he has been raised from the dead.”

06:17 For Herod himself had sent men to arrest John and chain him in prison on account of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife, whom Herod had married.

06:18 For John had been saying to Herod, “It is not lawful for you to have your brother’s wife.”

06:19 So Herodias held a grudge against John and wanted to kill him, but she was not able to do so.

06:20 because Herod feared John, knowing that he was a righteous and holy man, and he protected him. When he heard him, he was greatly perplexed, yet he heard him gladly.

06:21 But an opportunity came when Herod on his birthday gave a banquet for his nobles, military commanders, and the prominent men of Galilee.

06:22 When the daughter of Herodias came in and danced, she pleased Herod and his dinner guests. The king said to the girl, “Ask me for whatever you wish, and I will give it to you.”

06:23 He swore to her, “Whatever you ask me, I will give you, up to half of my kingdom.”

06:24 So she went out and said to her mother, “What should I ask for?” Her mother said, “The head of John the Baptist.”

06:25 Immediately the girl hurried back to the king and made her request: “I want you to give me at once the head of John the Baptist on a platter.”

06:26 The king was greatly distressed, but because of his oaths and his dinner guests, he did not want to refuse her.

06:27 So the king immediately sent an executioner and commanded that John’s head be brought. The man went and beheaded John in the prison,

06:28 brought his head on a platter, and gave it to the girl. The girl then gave it to her mother.

06:29 When John’s disciples heard about this, they came and took his body and laid it in a tomb.


06:30 The apostles gathered around Jesus and reported to him all that they had done and taught.

06:31 He said to them, “Come away by yourselves to a remote place and rest a while.” For many people were coming and going, and they did not even have a chance to eat.

06:32 So they went away by themselves in a boat to a remote place.

06:33 But many saw them leaving and recognized them, and they ran together on foot from all the towns and arrived there ahead of them.

06:34 When Jesus went ashore and saw a large crowd, he had compassion on them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd. So he began to teach them many things.

06:35 When it was already late in the day, his disciples came to him and said, “This place is remote, and it is already late.

06:36 Send the people away so that they can go to the surrounding countryside and villages and buy themselves something to eat.”

06:37 But he answered them, “You give them something to eat.” They said to him, “Should we go and buy two hundred denarii worth of bread and give it to them to eat?”

06:38 He said to them, “How many loaves do you have? Go and see.” When they found out, they said, “Five, and two fish.”

06:39 Then he directed them all to sit down in groups on the green grass.

06:40 So they sat down in groups of hundreds and fifties.

06:41 He took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, he blessed and broke the loaves. Then he gave them to his disciples to set before the people, and he divided the two fish among them all.

06:42 They all ate and were satisfied,

06:43 and the disciples picked up twelve baskets full of broken pieces and of the fish.

06:44 There were five thousand men who had eaten the loaves.


06:45 Immediately Jesus made his disciples get into the boat and go on ahead of him to Bethsaida, while he dismissed the crowd.

06:46 After taking leave of them, he went up on the mountain to pray.

06:47 When evening came, the boat was in the middle of the sea, and he was alone on the land.

06:48 He saw that they were straining at the oars, because the wind was against them. Shortly before dawn he came toward them, walking on the sea, and intended to pass by them.

06:49 When they saw him walking on the sea, they thought he was a ghost and cried out,

06:50 because they all saw him and were terrified. But Jesus immediately spoke to them: “Take courage! It is I. Do not be afraid.”

06:51 Then he got into the boat with them, and the wind ceased. They were utterly astounded,

06:52 for they had not understood about the loaves; their hearts were hardened.


06:53 When they had crossed over, they came to land at Gennesaret and anchored there.

06:54 As soon as they got out of the boat, people recognized Jesus.

06:55 They ran throughout that whole region and began to carry the sick on mats to wherever they heard he was.

06:56 And wherever he went—into villages, towns, or countryside—they laid the sick in the marketplaces and begged him to let them touch even the edge of his cloak. And all who touched it were healed.


07:01 The Pharisees and some of the scribes who had come from Jerusalem gathered around Jesus.

07:02 They saw some of his disciples eating bread with defiled (that is, unwashed) hands.

07:03 (For the Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they give their hands a ceremonial washing, holding to the tradition of the elders.

07:04 When they come from the marketplace, they do not eat unless they wash. And there are many other traditions that they observe, such as the washing of cups, pitchers, kettles, and dining couches.)

07:05 So the Pharisees and scribes asked Jesus, “Why do your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat bread with unwashed hands?”

07:06 He answered them, “Isaiah prophesied correctly about you hypocrites, as it is written: ‘These people honor me with their lips, but their heart is far from me.

07:07 They worship me in vain, teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’

07:08 You abandon the commandment of God and hold to the tradition of men.”

07:09 He went on to say, “You have a fine way of setting aside the commandment of God in order to keep your tradition!

07:10 For Moses said, ‘Honor your father and your mother,’ and, ‘Whoever speaks evil of father or mother must surely die.’

07:20 And he said, “What comes out of a person is what defiles him.

07:21 For from within, out of people’s hearts, come evil thoughts, sexual immorality, theft, murder,

07:29 Then he said to her, “Because of this reply, you may go. The demon has left your daughter.”

09:19 Jesus answered them, “You unbelieving generation, how long will I be with you? How long will I put up with you? Bring him to me.”

09:27 But Jesus took him by the hand and raised him to his feet, and he stood up.

09:28 After Jesus had gone into the house, his disciples asked him privately, “Why couldn’t we drive it out?”

09:29 He told them, “This kind can come out only by prayer.”

09:37 “Whoever welcomes one of these little children in my name welcomes me; and whoever welcomes me does not welcome me, but the one who sent me.”

09:41 Truly I tell you, anyone who gives you a cup of water to drink because you belong to Christ will certainly not lose his reward.

09:42 “But if anyone causes one of these little ones who believe in me to stumble, it would be better for him if a large millstone were hung around his neck and he were thrown into the sea.

09:43 If your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life maimed than to have two hands and go into hell, into the unquenchable fire,

09:44 where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched.

09:45 And if your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter life lame than to have two feet and be thrown into hell,

09:46 where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched.

09:47 And if your eye causes you to stumble, tear it out. It is better for you to enter the kingdom of God with one eye than to have two eyes and be thrown into hell,

09:48 where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched.

09:49 For everyone will be salted with fire.

09:50 Salt is good, but if the salt loses its flavor, how can you season it? Have salt among yourselves, and be at peace with one another.”

10:02 Some Pharisees came to test him. They asked, “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife?”

10:03 He replied, “What did Moses command you?”

10:04 They said, “Moses permitted a man to write a certificate of divorce and send her away.”

10:05 “It was because of your hardness of heart that he wrote you this command,” Jesus replied.

10:06 “But from the beginning of creation, God ‘made them male and female.’

10:07 ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife,

10:08 and the two will become one flesh.’ So they are no longer two, but one flesh.

10:09 Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate.”

10:11 He answered, “Whoever divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery against her.

10:18 “Why do you call me good?” Jesus answered. “No one is good except God alone.

10:19 You know the commandments: ‘Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not give false testimony, do not defraud, honor your father and mother.’”

10:20 “Teacher,” he declared, “all these I have kept since I was a boy.”

10:21 Jesus looked at him and loved him. “One thing you lack,” he said. “Go, sell everything you have and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me.”

10:43 Not so with you. Instead, whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant,

10:45 For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”

12:05 He sent still another, and that one they killed. He sent many others; some of them they beat, others they killed.

13:12 “Brother will betray brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rebel against their parents and have them put to death.

13:13 Everyone will hate you because of me, but the one who stands firm to the end will be saved.

13:14 “When you see ‘the abomination that causes desolation’ standing where it does not belong—let the reader understand—then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains.

13:15 Let no one on the housetop go down or enter the house to take anything out.

13:16 Let no one in the field go back to get their cloak.

13:17 How dreadful it will be in those days for pregnant women and nursing mothers!

13:18 Pray that this will not take place in winter,

13:19 because those will be days of distress unequaled from the beginning, when God created the world, until now—and never to be equaled again.

13:20 “If the Lord had not cut short those days, no one would survive. But for the sake of the elect, whom he has chosen, he has shortened them.

13:21 At that time if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Messiah!’ or, ‘Look, there he is!’ do not believe it.

13:22 For false messiahs and false prophets will appear and perform signs and wonders to deceive, if possible, even the elect.

13:23 So be on your guard; I have told you everything ahead of time.

13:24 “But in those days, following that distress, ‘the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light;

13:25 the stars will fall from the sky, and the heavenly bodies will be shaken.’

13:26 “At that time people will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.

13:27 And he will send his angels and gather his elect from the four winds, from the ends of the earth to the ends of the heavens.

13:28 “Now learn this lesson from the fig tree: As soon as its twigs get tender and its leaves come out, you know that summer is near.

13:29 Even so, when you see these things happening, you know that it is near, right at the door.

13:30 Truly I tell you, this generation will certainly not pass away until all these things have happened.

13:31 Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.


13:32 “But about that day or hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father.

13:33 Be on guard! Be alert! You do not know when that time will come.

13:34 It’s like a man going away: He leaves his house and puts his servants in charge, each with their assigned task, and tells the one at the door to keep watch.

13:35 “Therefore keep watch because you do not know when the owner of the house will come back—whether in the evening, or at midnight, or when the rooster crows, or at dawn.

13:36 If he comes suddenly, do not let him find you sleeping.

13:37 What I say to you, I say to everyone: ‘Watch!’”


14:01 Now the Passover and the Festival of Unleavened Bread were only two days away, and the chief priests and the teachers of the law were scheming to arrest Jesus secretly and kill him.

14:02 “But not during the festival,” they said, “or the people may riot.”


14:03 While he was in Bethany, reclining at the table in the home of Simon the Leper, a woman came with an alabaster jar of very expensive perfume, made of pure nard. She broke the jar and poured the perfume on his head.

14:04 Some of those present were saying indignantly to one another, “Why this waste of perfume?

14:05 It could have been sold for more than a year’s wages and the money given to the poor.” And they rebuked her harshly.

14:06 “Leave her alone,” said Jesus. “Why are you bothering her? She has done a beautiful thing to me.

14:07 The poor you will always have with you, and you can help them any time you want. But you will not always have me.

14:08 She did what she could. She poured perfume on my body beforehand to prepare for my burial.

14:09 Truly I tell you, wherever the gospel is preached throughout the world, what she has done will also be told, in memory of her.”

14:10 Then Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, went to the chief priests to betray Jesus to them.

14:11 They were delighted to hear this and promised to give him money. So he watched for an opportunity to hand him over.


14:12 On the first day of the Festival of Unleavened Bread, when it was customary to sacrifice the Passover lamb, Jesus’ disciples asked him, “Where do you want us to go and make preparations for you to eat the Passover?”

14:13 So he sent two of his disciples, telling them, “Go into the city, and a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him.

14:14 Say to the owner of the house he enters, ‘The Teacher asks: Where is my guest room, where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?’

14:15 He will show you a large room upstairs, furnished and ready. Make preparations for us there.”

14:16 The disciples left, went into the city and found things just as Jesus had told them. So they prepared the Passover.

14:17 When evening came, Jesus arrived with the Twelve.

14:18 While they were reclining at the table eating, he said, “Truly I tell you, one of you will betray me—one who is eating with me.”

14:19 They were saddened, and one by one they said to him, “Surely you don’t mean me?”

14:20 “It is one of the Twelve,” he replied, “one who dips bread into the bowl with me.

14:21 The Son of Man will go just as it is written about him. But woe to that man who betrays the Son of Man! It would be better for him if he had not been born.”


14:22 While they were eating, Jesus took bread, and when he had given thanks, he broke it and gave it to his disciples, saying, “Take it; this is my body.”

14:23 Then he took a cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave it to them, and they all drank from it.

14:24 “This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many,” he said to them.

14:25 “Truly I tell you, I will not drink again from the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God.”


14:26 When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.


14:27 “You will all fall away,” Jesus told them, “for it is written: ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.’

14:28 But after I have risen, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”

14:29 Peter declared, “Even if all fall away, I will not.”

14:30 “Truly I tell you,” Jesus answered, “today—yes, tonight—before the rooster crows twice you yourself will disown me three times.”

14:31 But Peter insisted emphatically, “Even if I have to die with you, I will never disown you.” And all the others said the same.


14:32 They went to a place called Gethsemane, and Jesus said to his disciples, “Sit here while I pray.”

14:33 He took Peter, James and John along with him, and he began to be deeply distressed and troubled.

14:34 “My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death,” he said to them. “Stay here and keep watch.”

14:35 Going a little farther, he fell to the ground and prayed that if possible the hour might pass from him.

14:36 “Abba, Father,” he said, “everything is possible for you. Take this cup from me. Yet not what I will, but what you will.”

14:37 Then he returned to his disciples and found them sleeping. “Simon,” he said to Peter, “are you asleep? Couldn’t you keep watch for one hour?

14:38 Watch and pray so that you will not fall into temptation. The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak.”

14:39 Once more he went away and prayed the same thing.

14:40 When he came back, he again found them sleeping, because their eyes were heavy. They did not know what to say to him.

14:41 Returning the third time, he said to them, “Are you still sleeping and resting? Enough! The hour has come. Look, the Son of Man is delivered into the hands of sinners.

14:42 Rise! Let us go! Here comes my betrayer!”


14:43 Just as he was speaking, Judas, one of the Twelve, appeared. With him was a crowd armed with swords and clubs, sent from the chief priests, the teachers of the law, and the elders.

14:44 Now the betrayer had arranged a signal with them: “The one I kiss is the man; arrest him and lead him away under guard.”

14:45 Going at once to Jesus, Judas said, “Rabbi!” and kissed him.

14:46 The men seized Jesus and arrested him.

14:47 Then one of those standing near drew his sword and struck the servant of the high priest, cutting off his ear.

14:48 “Am I leading a rebellion,” said Jesus, “that you have come out with swords and clubs to capture me?

14:49 Every day I was with you, teaching in the temple courts, and you did not arrest me. But the Scriptures must be fulfilled.”

14:50 Then everyone deserted him and fled.

14:51 A young man, wearing nothing but a linen garment, was following Jesus. When they seized him,

14:52 he fled naked, leaving his garment behind.


14:53 They took Jesus to the high priest, and all the chief priests, the elders and the teachers of the law came together.

14:54 Peter followed him at a distance, right into the courtyard of the high priest. There he sat with the guards and warmed himself at the fire.

14:55 The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were looking for evidence against Jesus so that they could put him to death, but they did not find any.

14:56 Many testified falsely against him, but their statements did not agree.

14:57 Then some stood up and gave this false testimony against him:

14:58 “We heard him say, ‘I will destroy this temple made with human hands and in three days will build another, not made with hands.’”

14:59 Yet even then their testimony did not agree.

14:60 Then the high priest stood up before them and asked Jesus, “Are you not going to answer? What is this testimony that these men are bringing against you?”

14:61 But Jesus remained silent and gave no answer. Again the high priest asked him, “Are you the Messiah, the Son of the Blessed One?”

14:62 “I am,” said Jesus. “And you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Mighty One and coming on the clouds of heaven.”

14:63 The high priest tore his clothes. “Why do we need any more witnesses?” he asked.

14:64 “You have heard the blasphemy. What do you think?” They all condemned him as worthy of death.

14:65 Then some began to spit at him; they blindfolded him, struck him with their fists, and said, “Prophesy!” And the guards took him and beat him.


14:66 While Peter was below in the courtyard, one of the servant girls of the high priest came by.

14:67 When she saw Peter warming himself, she looked closely at him. “You also were with that Nazarene, Jesus,” she said.

14:68 But he denied it. “I don’t know or understand what you’re talking about,” he said, and he went out into the entryway. And the rooster crowed.

14:69 When the servant girl saw him there, she said again to those standing around, “This fellow is one of them.”

14:70 Again he denied it. After a little while, those standing near said to Peter, “Surely you are one of them, for you are a Galilean.”

14:71 He began to call down curses, and he swore to them, “I don’t know this man you’re talking about.”

14:72 Immediately the rooster crowed the second time. Then Peter remembered the word Jesus had spoken to him: “Before the rooster crows twice you will disown me three times.” And he broke down and wept.


15:01 Very early in the morning, the chief priests, with the elders, the teachers of the law and the whole Sanhedrin, made their plans. So they bound Jesus, led him away and handed him over to Pilate.

15:02 “Are you the king of the Jews?” asked Pilate. “You have said so,” Jesus replied.

15:03 The chief priests accused him of many things.

15:04 So again Pilate asked him, “Aren’t you going to answer? See how many things they are accusing you of.”

15:05 But Jesus still made no reply, and Pilate was amazed.


15:06 Now it was the custom at the festival to release a prisoner whom the people requested.

15:07 A man called Barabbas was in prison with the insurrectionists who had committed murder in the uprising.

15:08 The crowd came up and asked Pilate to do for them what he usually did.

15:09 “Do you want me to release to you the king of the Jews?” asked Pilate,

15:10 knowing it was out of self-interest that the chief priests had handed Jesus over to him.

15:11 But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to have Pilate release Barabbas instead.

15:12 “What shall I do, then, with the one you call the king of the Jews?” Pilate asked them.

15:13 “Crucify him!” they shouted.

15:14 “Why? What crime has he committed?” asked Pilate. But they shouted all the louder, “Crucify him!”

15:15 Wanting to satisfy the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas to them. He had Jesus flogged, and handed him over to be crucified.


15:16 The soldiers led Jesus away into the palace (that is, the Praetorium) and called together the whole company of soldiers.

15:17 They put a purple robe on him, then twisted together a crown of thorns and set it on him.

15:18 And they began to call out to him, “Hail, king of the Jews!”

15:19 Again and again they struck him on the head with a staff and spit on him. Falling on their knees, they paid homage to him.

15:20 And when they had mocked him, they took off the purple robe and put his own clothes on him. Then they led him out to crucify him.


15:21 A certain man from Cyrene, Simon, the father of Alexander and Rufus, was passing by on his way in from the country, and they forced him to carry the cross.

15:22 They brought Jesus to the place called Golgotha (which means “the place of the skull”).

15:23 Then they offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not take it.

15:24 And they crucified him. Dividing up his clothes, they cast lots to see what each would get.

15:25 It was nine in the morning when they crucified him.

15:26 The written notice of the charge against him read: THE KING OF THE JEWS.

15:27 They crucified two rebels with him, one on his right and one on his left.

15:28 (And the scripture was fulfilled that says, “He was counted with the lawless.”)

15:29 Those who passed by hurled insults at him, shaking their heads and saying, “So! You who are going to destroy the temple and build it in three days,

15:30 come down from the cross and save yourself!”

15:31 In the same way the chief priests and the teachers of the law mocked him among themselves. “He saved others,” they said, “but he can’t save himself!

15:32 Let this Messiah, this king of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe.” Those crucified with him also heaped insults on him.


15:33 At noon, darkness came over the whole land until three in the afternoon.

15:34 And at three in the afternoon Jesus cried out in a loud voice, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” (which means “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”).

15:35 When some of those standing near heard this, they said, “Listen, he’s calling Elijah.”

15:36 Someone ran, filled a sponge with wine vinegar, put it on a staff, and offered it to Jesus to drink. “Now leave him alone. Let’s see if Elijah comes to take him down,” he said.

15:37 With a loud cry, Jesus breathed his last.

15:38 The curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom.

15:39 And when the centurion, who stood there in front of Jesus, saw how he died, he said, “Surely this man was the Son of God!”

15:40 Some women were watching from a distance. Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joseph, and Salome.

15:41 In Galilee these women had followed him and cared for his needs. Many other women who had come up with him to Jerusalem were also there.


15:42 It was Preparation Day (that is, the day before the Sabbath). So as evening approached,

15:43 Joseph of Arimathea, a prominent member of the Council, who was himself waiting for the kingdom of God, went boldly to Pilate and asked for Jesus’ body.

15:44 Pilate was surprised to hear that he was already dead. Summoning the centurion, he asked him if Jesus had already died.

15:45 When he learned from the centurion that it was so, he gave the body to Joseph.

15:46 So Joseph bought some linen cloth, took down the body, wrapped it in the linen, and placed it in a tomb cut out of rock. Then he rolled a stone against the entrance of the tomb.

15:47 Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joseph saw where he was laid.


16:01 When the Sabbath was over, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought spices so that they might go to anoint Jesus’ body.

16:02 Very early on the first day of the week, just after sunrise, they were on their way to the tomb

16:03 and they asked each other, “Who will roll the stone away from the entrance of the tomb?”

16:04 But when they looked up, they saw that the stone, which was very large, had been rolled away.

16:05 As they entered the tomb, they saw a young man dressed in a white robe sitting on the right side, and they were alarmed.

16:06 “Don’t be alarmed,” he said. “You are looking for Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He has risen! He is not here. See the place where they laid him.

16:07 But go, tell his disciples and Peter, ‘He is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see him, just as he told you.’”

16:08 Trembling and bewildered, the women went out and fled from the tomb. They said nothing to anyone, because they were afraid.


16:09 When Jesus rose early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had driven seven demons.

16:10 She went and told those who had been with him and who were mourning and weeping.

16:11 When they heard that Jesus was alive and that she had seen him, they did not believe it.

16:12 Afterward Jesus appeared in a different form to two of them while they were walking in the country.

16:13 These returned and reported it to the rest; but they did not believe them either.

16:14 Later Jesus appeared to the Eleven as they were eating; he rebuked them for their lack of faith and their stubborn refusal to believe those who had seen him after he had risen.

16:15 He said to them, “Go into all the world and preach the gospel to all creation.

16:16 Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned.

16:17 And these signs will accompany those who believe: In my name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new tongues;

16:18 they will pick up snakes with their hands; and when they drink deadly poison, it will not hurt them at all; they will place their hands on sick people, and they will get well.”

16:19 After the Lord Jesus had spoken to them, he was taken up into heaven and he sat at the right hand of God.

16:20 Then the disciples went out and preached everywhere, and the Lord worked with them and confirmed his word by the signs that accompanied it. Amen.
